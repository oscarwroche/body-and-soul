Loop the phrase

Drop in a recording, grab the A/B handles, and slow playback to 50% while keeping the original pitch.

Write the part

Enter notes and rests with the mouse or keyboard. Choose a key and meter, and scroll the score independently of playback.

Keep your work

Reopen a local project with your score and loop settings. Export a PDF when you want to print the part.

Install on your Mac

  1. Double-click the downloaded .dmg. If a window doesn’t open, select Body and Soul 1.3.10 under Locations in Finder.
  2. Drag Body and Soul into the Applications shortcut.
  3. Open Body and Soul from Applications.

Already installed? Choose Body and Soul → Check for Updates… from the menu bar.

The beta is unnotarized. If macOS blocks the first launch, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway for the app. Apple’s first-launch guide
